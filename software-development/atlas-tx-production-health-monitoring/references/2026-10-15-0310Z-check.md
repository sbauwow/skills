- Waiter-wedge triage order: (1) wrapper + child `sleep` alive? (2) journalctl `PM: suspend entry/exit` total freeze since launch (`last -x` does NOT show suspend episodes), (3) projected wake = clock wake + freeze, (4) only call it wedged if projected wake is in the PAST with the child still sleeping. Before that, assume on-track and never re-arm.
